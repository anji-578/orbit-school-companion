## Fair-use limits (to keep service fast & affordable)

### Both tiers
| Limit | Policy |
|--|--|
| Active students | Priced headcount = students with login enabled |
| Storage (notes, vault) | **500 MB / student** soft cap; school pool reviewed yearly |
| Push / in-app alerts | Unlimited operational alerts (attendance, homework, broadcasts) |
| Support | Email / WhatsApp school desk · business hours · 1 named admin contact |

### Pro only — AI & Personal Development
| Limit | Fair use |
|--|--|
| **AI Study Copilot** | Up to **20 messages / student / day** |
| **Paper Scan** | Up to **5 scans / student / week** · images auto-deleted after **14 days** |
| **Personal Development Plan** | 1 active plan / student · goals & check-ins inside Orbit · AI coaching counts toward daily AI messages |
| Abuse / sharing accounts | School may be asked to remediate; repeated abuse may throttle AI for that tenant |

Basic has **no** AI / Paper Scan / Personal Development Plan entitlements.  
Upgrading a student Basic → Pro is prorated for the remaining academic year (school admin request).
