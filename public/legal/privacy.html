Orbit Privacy Policy

Effective: 14 November 2026 · Version 1.0 · For school students in India

Orbit (“we”) provides a school learning companion for students, parents, teachers, and school staff. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how schools and families can exercise rights under applicable Indian law (including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 principles we engineer toward).

1. Who controls the data

Your school is the primary controller of student education records (roster, attendance, grades, homework). Orbit processes that data on the school’s instructions as a processor / service provider. Contact your school office for most access or correction requests.

2. What we collect

  • Account: email, display name, role (student / parent / teacher / school).
  • School records synced for your class: timetable, homework, attendance, grades, notices.
  • App usage needed to run the product: device session, crash diagnostics (Sentry, if enabled), AI tutor prompts you submit.
  • Optional: push notification token if you enable alerts.

We do not sell personal data. We do not use student data for advertising.

3. Why we process data

  • learning_support — homework help, Ask Orbit, study tools.
  • operations — roster sync, school workflows.
  • product_quality — reliability and defect diagnosis.
  • safety — safeguarding signals required by school policy.

4. Children and guardians

Orbit is used by school-age students under school onboarding. Guardians should review school consent forms. Students can limit profile visibility in-app; school records still follow school permissions.

5. Retention

Education records follow the school’s retention schedule. App diagnostics are kept only as long as needed for product quality. After account deletion / anonymization by the school, personal identifiers are removed or irreversibly anonymized where technically feasible.

6. Sharing

Infrastructure vendors: Supabase (database/auth), Vercel (hosting/API), Google Gemini via our authenticated server proxy (prompts you send to Orbit AI), and optionally Sentry (crashes). We do not share student data with advertisers.

7. Your rights

  • Export your Orbit-held student package in Settings → Profile visibility / Your data.
  • Request account deletion via Delete account or in-app Settings. Schools complete erasure of official records.
  • Contact: privacy@orbit.app

8. Security

Access is role-based with database row-level security. AI calls require a signed-in session on production. The Android app disables cleartext HTTP and cloud backup of app data.

9. Changes

We will update this page and the in-app policy version when material changes occur.
