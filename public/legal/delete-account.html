Delete your Orbit account

Google Play account-deletion disclosure · Version 1.0

You can request deletion of your Orbit app account and associated personal identifiers. School official records (attendance registers, mark sheets) are controlled by your school and may be retained under school policy after app access is removed.

In the Android app (preferred)

  1. Open Orbit → Me → Settings → Profile visibility.
  2. Tap Request account deletion.
  3. Confirm. We log the request for your school and sign you out of this device.

By email

Email privacy@orbit.app from your school-registered address with subject “Orbit account deletion” and your full name + class. We acknowledge within 2 business days and complete app-account removal within 30 days (often sooner with school confirmation).

What is deleted

  • Orbit sign-in link for your profile (after school confirmation / anonymization).
  • In-app consents and learner XP identity tied to your student row where policy allows.
  • Local app data on devices you control (clear app storage / uninstall).

What may remain

  • School-mandated education records.
  • Security audit logs without unnecessary personal detail.
  • Backups until rotated per retention schedule.
